Privacy

Website privacy notice.

This notice describes information handled through the public Ables website and product-inquiry process. Customer deployments are governed by their applicable agreements.

Effective August 9, 2026

On this page Scope Information Analytics How it is used Providers Your choices Contact

Scope

This notice applies to ables.ai and information you send when requesting a demo or contacting Ables. It does not replace a customer agreement, data-processing agreement, or privacy notice for a separately deployed customer service.

Information submitted or generated

The demo form can collect your name, work email, company, role, monthly funded-volume range, and the message you choose to provide. A submitted request can also include the landing-page path, referring-page path, UTM campaign parameters, the demo button used, and a Google Analytics client identifier so Ables can understand which marketing activity produced the inquiry. If you email Ables, the message and contact information associated with that email are received as well.

The site stores a light-or-dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage. Hosting, content-delivery, and security infrastructure may also receive routine technical information such as IP address, requested URL, browser type, timestamps, and response status.

Do not send customer financial data through the demo form.

Do not submit bank statements, account credentials, government identifiers, or other sensitive customer records unless Ables has provided an approved channel and the necessary agreement is in place.

Website analytics

Ables uses Google Analytics 4 on the public marketing, solution, integration, and resource pages listed in the public sitemap. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers to measure page views and interactions and may receive information such as the page and referring URL, browser and device details, approximate location, and interaction events. Those events can include opening the demo form, clicking the product-demo video, and a confirmed demo-request submission. Ables does not send the form’s name, email, company, role, volume, or message fields to Google Analytics. Ables uses the resulting aggregate reports to understand how these pages are used and to improve their content and performance.

The workflow readiness assessment calculates its result in your browser. The values entered into the assessment are not submitted to Ables, sent to Google Analytics, stored in local storage, or included in the page URL by the assessment tool.

Google provides the analytics service and handles analytics information under its own practices. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy. You can delete or block cookies through your browser, use supported browser privacy controls, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. These controls may not prevent routine hosting or security logs described above.

The Google Analytics tag described here is not installed on the 29 legacy, test, or tool pages currently available on this domain. Those pages may use separate providers or technologies based on the service they support.

How information is used

Information submitted through the public site may be used to respond to inquiries, arrange a product discussion, understand requested workflows, maintain site security, diagnose errors, and keep a record of business communications.

Retention can vary with the nature of the inquiry, security needs, and applicable obligations. Contact Ables to ask about the period applicable to a specific record or to request deletion.

Service providers and linked services

The website relies on providers for hosting, content delivery, and analytics. Public pages serve their web-font files from ables.ai. The demo request is sent to an Ables service hosted at 2.ables.ai. Legacy or tool pages may load separate authentication, analytics, document, or user-interface libraries; those pages are excluded from the public search index but remain accessible to intended users.

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, email providers, and other external services take you to services governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices and requests

You can avoid submitting the demo form and contact Ables by email instead. You can clear the stored theme preference through your browser settings. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how personal information is handled.

To make a request, email the address below. Ables may need enough information to identify the relevant record and verify the request.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the public website or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

Contact

Send privacy questions or requests to zak@ables.ai. For security reports, see Security & Trust.
